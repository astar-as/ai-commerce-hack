06  —  Product choices

Every choice has a reason.

01 · VOICE-FIRST
Talk, don’t type.
Hands are full — cooking, pushing a cart. Saying the dish is the fastest input there is.
02 · SPLIT BRAIN
Voice talks. ZooWork thinks.
GPT-Live keeps the voice instant; the ZooWork agent gets time to reason and call tools.
03 · ONE SCREEN
Mobile web, no install.
A shopper opens a link. A grocer can ship it on Monday.
04 · REAL DATA
One real Kroger store.
4,014 products with real aisles, stock, prices and allergens beat a synthetic demo.
05 · SPEED
No LLM in the hot path.
“Not on the shelf” → Moss → in-stock alternatives in ~2 ms, even on store wifi.
06 · MARGIN
Store brand first.
The substitute that keeps the sale usually earns the grocer more per item.
07 · SAFETY
Safety in the tools.
Not in the prompt: allergens and diet are filtered before the agent ever sees a product.
08 · TRUST
Order state in our backend.
The screen renders what’s true — not what the model remembers.
09 · REACH
Instacart + Kroger.
Instacart link for delivery from any retailer; Kroger for pickup, aisles and stock.
